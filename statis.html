Rice Productivity Estimation using Hyperspectral UAV Imagery

IndividualThesisBlackBird V2ISE-PLS

National rice consumption is projected to reach 31.7 million tons by 2045 while production dropped 2.05% to 30.90 million tons (2023). Conventional estimation methods fail to capture spatial variability in productivity efficiently and precisely.

Method

ISE-PLS (Iterative Stepwise Elimination - Partial Least Squares) on BlackBird V2 hyperspectral UAV imagery (100 bands). Acquired at flowering (64 DAS) and grain-filling (73 DAS). Bands red-edge (755-760 nm) and NIR (930-935 nm) were identified as dominant.

Acquisition

ISE-PLS reduced 100 bands to 8 bands without significant accuracy loss (R²=0.8335). Spatial productivity estimates of 0.391-0.634 kg/m² reflect the influence of terraced irrigation and micro-topography.

Impact

ISE-PLS reduced 100 bands to 8 bands without significant accuracy loss (R²=0.8335). Spatial productivity estimates of 0.391-0.634 kg/m² reflect the influence of terraced irrigation and micro-topography. Graduated with Highly Satisfactory · July 2025.
